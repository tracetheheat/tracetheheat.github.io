Fare-free public transport (FFPT) was my first major research theme and the subject of my doctoral work. Over several years I examined why Polish and Czech municipalities abolish fares, how these policies are distributed geographically, who they reach, and what happens to the politics of urban mobility once the fare disappears.

A detailed project description and the full list of related publications are being prepared.
