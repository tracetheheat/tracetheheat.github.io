*Examining Spatial Access to Addiction Treatment Provision Across Poland*

Research on the geography of drug demand in Poland and Central and Eastern Europe is well developed. The supply side is not: we know remarkably little about where addiction treatment facilities are located, who can reach them, and how national policy frameworks shape that distribution. This project addresses that gap.

The work examines three dimensions: the spatial organisation of treatment provision across Polish territories and communities; equitable access and treatment-seeking experiences among vulnerable populations; and the role of policymaking and healthcare governance in producing spatial (in)equity. It combines quantitative spatial analysis with stakeholder interviews, focus groups and policy document analysis.

The project runs from October 2026 over 36 months and is funded by the National Science Centre, Poland (OPUS 30).
