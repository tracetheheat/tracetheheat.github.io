I'm a transport and social geographer. Two themes anchor my work and each has its own page. [Fare-free public transport](/projects/fare-free-public-transport/) is the one I spent a decade on; the project has ended, my interest in it has not. [Geographies of Recovery](/projects/geographies-of-recovery/), which I currently lead, examines spatial access to addiction treatment provision across Poland.

Alongside them I work on shared mobility and e-scooter sharing, food waste mitigation and food-sharing platforms, and conflicts over transport policy in European cities. An overview of both projects is on the [projects page](/projects/). Everything I have published is listed below.
