&#9995; Curious about what I do when I'm not doing research? Or about what helps me **recharge my battery?**

I run, most days. Most of my racing happens over the half marathon, where I've gone under 80 minutes, and the marathon, where I've recently dipped under three hours. Every so often I flirt with longer distances, usually timed races on a loop, where the clock decides when you stop rather than the finish line. Kenenisa Bekele has nothing to worry about, but the miles do their job: they clear my head. If you run too, you can follow me on [Strava](https://www.strava.com/athletes/straubd) &#127939;

{% include image.html url="/images/me2.png" caption="" width=180 align="center" %}

I also write outside academia. At [CZECHTAPE](https://czechtape.substack.com/) I cover Czech and Slovak alternative music for Polish readers, with reviews, interviews and longer pieces on the scene. It is written in Polish.

**You still here?** Then have a look at my [blog](/blog/) &#9749;, where I think through things connected to my research, or at my [Goodreads profile](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/179261329-daniel-straub) if you are a book person too.
