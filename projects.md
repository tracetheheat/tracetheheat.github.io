Alongside my broader work on shared mobility and transport diplomacy, two projects have shaped my research agenda.

[**Fare-free public transport**](/projects/fare-free-public-transport/) — my first major research theme, examining the adoption, geography and social effects of fare-free public transport policy in Poland and Czechia. Completed.

[**Geographies of Recovery**](/projects/geographies-of-recovery/) — spatial access to addiction treatment provision across Poland. Ongoing, 2026–2029.
